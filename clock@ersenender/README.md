# Clock

A clock with the weekday and date below it. Choose between an analog dial (Arabic, Eastern Arabic or Roman numerals, or marks only) and a digital display (24 or 12 hours), with or without seconds, optionally with the calendar week.

- **Timer**: right-click the desklet and pick 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes or your own length. The remaining time is shown below the clock and as an arc on the dial. A running timer survives a restart of Cinnamon.
- **Alarm**: a daily alarm time, every day or Monday to Friday only, marked on the dial.
- Both ring with a sound, a notification and a highlighted line that you click to dismiss. Sound and notification can be switched off.

## Settings

Right-click the desklet and choose *Configure*. Besides the options above you can choose the accent colour (seven presets or your own) and the colour of the card.

## Languages

English, German and Turkish. Further translations are welcome: the template is `files/clock@ersenender/po/clock@ersenender.pot`.
