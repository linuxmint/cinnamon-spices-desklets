# Notes and Calls

Sticky notes for the desktop, plus **call notes**: who called, the phone number, what it was about, and whether they want to be called back. Open callbacks are counted at the top and can be ticked off with one click.

The yellow plus adds a note, the phone button a call note; click a note to edit it (save with Ctrl+Enter). The notes are stored in `~/.local/state/notes-and-calls@ersenender/notizen.json`.

Requires `python3` with GTK 3 bindings (`python3-gi`), preinstalled on Linux Mint.

## Settings

Right-click the desklet and choose *Configure*.

## Languages

English, German and Turkish. Further translations are welcome: the template is `files/notes-and-calls@ersenender/po/notes-and-calls@ersenender.pot`.
