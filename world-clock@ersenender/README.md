# World Time

Two to eight analog clocks side by side, each set to a city of your choice (58 cities and UTC), with the time in digits (24 or 12 hours) and the difference to your local time below each clock. The clock for your own time zone is highlighted; each clock can get its own label.

- **Timeline**: the next 24 hours per place - working hours, waking hours and night - with a line on top for the hours when it is daytime everywhere. Handy for finding a time to call.
- **Shift the time** with the mouse wheel over the desklet (30-minute steps) to see what time it will be elsewhere; a click on *Back to now* or two minutes of rest return to the present.
- **Clock changes**: when a time difference changes within the next 30 days (daylight saving time), the date and the new difference are shown below the clock.

Everything is calculated locally from the system's time zone data; there is no network access.

## Settings

Right-click the desklet and choose *Configure*. Besides the options above you can choose the accent colour (seven presets or your own) and the colour of the card.

## Languages

English, German and Turkish. Further translations are welcome: the template is `files/world-clock@ersenender/po/world-clock@ersenender.pot`.
